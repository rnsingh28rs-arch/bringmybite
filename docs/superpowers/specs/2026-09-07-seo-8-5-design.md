# Bring My Bite SEO 8.5+ Design

## Goal
Improve crawlability, on-page relevance, structured data, social sharing, and indexable public content without changing the Supabase CMS, customer ordering flow, PWA behavior, or staff operations.

## Approach
Use a lightweight client-side SEO layer for route-specific metadata and JSON-LD, plus static `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml`. Add real public landing pages for high-intent meal-service topics and keep all transactional UI on the existing homepage/components.

## Public routes
- `/`
- `/monthly-meal-subscription`
- `/veg-meal-subscription`
- `/egg-meal-subscription`
- `/non-veg-meal-subscription`
- `/instant-thali`
- `/weekly-menu`
- `/how-it-works`
- `/delivery-areas`
- `/contact`

## Constraints
- Do not alter Supabase schemas or CMS source-of-truth behavior.
- Do not alter order lifecycle, payment, inventory, Admin, Chef, Manager, or D-Admin behavior.
- Do not invent service locations, ratings, reviews, prices, addresses, or business claims not present in the product.
- Keep staff routes out of sitemap and indexing directives.
- Use canonical HTTPS URLs on `bringmybite.com`.
- Schema must describe only content actually shown on the page.

## SEO features
1. Route-aware title, description, canonical, robots, Open Graph and Twitter metadata.
2. Organization + WebSite JSON-LD globally; Product/Offer and FAQ JSON-LD only where applicable.
3. Semantic H1/H2 content on landing pages targeting monthly meal subscription, veg/egg/non-veg meals, instant thali, weekly menu, delivery workflow, and contact/help intent.
4. Sitemap containing only public SEO routes.
5. Robots allowing public pages and explicitly disallowing staff portal paths.
6. Descriptive internal links between public landing pages.
7. Accessible headings, link labels, and image-independent content so pages remain useful without JavaScript imagery.
